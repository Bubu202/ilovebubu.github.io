Shiba inus are very stubborn creatures they don't care about what you say they follow what their hearts say. They are extremely cute and independent creatures. Somepeople call them the cats of dogs. Although, they still love attention. Shibas have many vocal sounds. their most iconic one is the shiba scream it's a high pitched mix of screaming, barking and yelping. Shiba inus range from 17-24 pounds but Bubu was likely mixed because he weighs 35 pounds. Shibas are also escape artists Bubu escaped from a room when the door was closed! Shibas like to chance small animals such as squirrels because of there hunting genetics. Shibas kind of look like foxes but are closely related to wolves.
