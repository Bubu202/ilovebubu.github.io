Shiba Inus are ancient Japanese hunting dogs that originated from over 2000 years ago. During WWII the Shiba inu breed almost went extinct due to a lack of food. Luckily the breed was saved by breeding the three types of Shiba Inus, San'in, Mino, and Shinshu shibas. These three breeds created the modern Shiba we see today.
